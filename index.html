Research rules

The scanner uses a 100-tick primary window with minimum 15% leading frequency, at least a 3 percentage-point lead over the second digit, and agreement from at least 3 of the 25/50/100/250-tick windows. The last 30% of the downloaded history is evaluated out-of-sample. A candidate must meet the minimum OOS signal count and exceed the 10% baseline. Weak setups are reported as HOLD rather than forced into a Matches recommendation.
Important: This is research software, not a profit guarantee. A historical frequency or OOS rate does not guarantee the next tick. Verify signals on fresh data and use appropriate risk controls.
