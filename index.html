How the recommendation works

1. Historical ticks are downloaded independently for all five indices. 2. The last 30% is treated as unseen out-of-sample data. 3. A Matches signal requires the primary 100-tick window to have at least 15% frequency, at least a 3-point lead over the second digit, and agreement from at least 3 of the 25/50/100/250-tick windows. 4. The displayed digit is the current consensus digit. 5. The scanner will say NO QUALIFIED SIGNAL rather than force a trade when the evidence is weak.
Important: A high historical percentage is not a guarantee of the next tick. Digit outcomes can be highly variable, and this tool does not promise an 80% win rate or guaranteed profit. Use the result as research and verify it on fresh data before risking money.
